# Getting Started

1. Install [Freighter](https://www.freighter.app/) and create or import a
   Stellar account. Mergepay defaults to **mainnet** (`public`), so for a
   free trial with no real money, switch Freighter to **testnet** and fund
   the account at [friendbot.stellar.org](https://friendbot.stellar.org)
   — and make sure the app you're using is also pointed at testnet.
2. Go to the Mergepay app and click **Sign in**. Freighter will prompt
   you to sign a login challenge — this is not a payment, it costs
   nothing and doesn't move funds, it just proves you control that
   account (this is SEP-10; see [How It Works](../introduction/how-it-works.md)).
3. Create a group, or accept an invite link from someone else's group
   (`/join/[code]`).
4. Add your first expense: amount, who paid, and how to split it.

That's the whole setup. There's no separate Mergepay account or password
— your Stellar wallet is your account, and your session token expires
after 12 hours, after which you sign a new challenge.
